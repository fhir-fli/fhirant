# FHIR ANT — closed-test instructions for testers

Send this to the people you add to the closed test. Fill in the two links from
the Play Console (the opt-in URL and the store URL it gives you).

Hi — thanks for helping test **FHIR ANT**.

I need at least ~12 people opted in to the test for 14 days so Google will let
me publish the app publicly. You don't have to actually use it — just install it
and leave it on your device for the two weeks. It takes two minutes:

1. On your Android phone, make sure you're signed in with the Google account you
   gave me (that's the account I added to the tester list).
2. Open this opt-in link and tap **Become a tester**:
   `<PASTE THE PLAY CONSOLE OPT-IN URL HERE>`
3. Then open the app's Play Store page and tap **Install**:
   `<PASTE THE STORE URL HERE>`
   (It may say "You're a tester" — that's expected.)
4. Please leave it installed until **<DATE = today + 14 days>**. That's all.

If you want to try it: open the app, tap **Start** on the server card, and it
runs a FHIR server on your phone that other devices on the same Wi‑Fi can
connect to. Loading the sample data (in onboarding, or later) fills it with
example patients to poke at. But this is optional — just having it installed is
what counts.

Questions → grey@fhirfli.dev

## For your own tracking (not for testers)
- Console → Testing → Closed testing → create a track, add tester emails (or a
  Google Group).
- The 14-day / ~12-tester clock starts once they're opted in; confirm the exact
  minimum tester count in the Console (Google has changed it).
- After 14 days with enough active testers, apply for production access, then
  promote the same AAB to Production for general availability.

## 1. Authorization (auth ON unless the row says otherwise)

The 2026-09-06 set put the compartment on the type-level search, read,
compartment routes and `$everything`. The same boundary is missing from
every other way of reaching a resource: the root path, Bundles, history,
system search, `$document`, `$evaluate`, conditional operations, includes,
and the body of a write. Rows 1–3 are the ones that matter most.

## 6. What is right and should stay

The 2026-09-06 fixes hold under this pass: scope intersection at issue,

backup with PBKDF2-210k/AES-GCM, the specification tagged in `resources`,

the JWT secret, the fhir_r4_bulk stream and models. The register/login/
refresh handlers, password policy, token hashing, secure storage identity
reuse and the logging sink were read and are clean.
